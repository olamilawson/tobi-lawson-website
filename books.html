WHO MADE THIS?

A microhistory of everyday things

Every morning, millions of people reach for an aluminum octagonal pot, twist a metallic tap, or click a ballpoint pen without considering the fierce industrial battles, accidental metallurgical discoveries, and human obsession engineered into those geometric forms.

Who Made This? traces the physical origins of modern domestic life. Moving object by object—from Alfonso Bialetti’s aluminum foundry in Piedmont to the early safety bicycle workshops of the 1890s—this volume recovers the sense of awe buried inside the ordinary objects surrounding us.
