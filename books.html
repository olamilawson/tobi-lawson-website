WHO MADE THIS?

A microhistory of everyday things

The stories behind 26 everyday objects, most of them part of daily life in Nigeria: the okada, the generator, garri, pure water, ankara, the jerry can and the plastic chair.

Each chapter starts with one object and follows it back through the people, accidents and trade that made it. Chapter One follows the okada, the motorcycle taxi named after an airline that folded in 1997.
