FREE COURSE • IN DEVELOPMENT

Artificial Intelligence in Frontier Markets

A free course on artificial intelligence for a general audience in Africa: how it works, who builds it, and what it means for work.

01 / COURSE OVERVIEW

You don't need a technical background. The course starts with the machines and data centres AI runs on, then the models at its centre and the products built on them.

The last two modules turn to work: which jobs AI disrupts, and where the openings are for Africans who want to build, invest or retrain.
