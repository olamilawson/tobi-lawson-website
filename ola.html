Ola

My personal agent. He prepares the work, and nothing leaves until I approve it.

What he does

01 / WHAT HE DOES

Sets out the day

Each morning he reads my calendar and my unread mail, then lists the meetings, the replies I owe, what falls due and what waits for my approval.

Drafts emails and invitations

If I ask him to write to someone or set up a meeting, he prepares it and stops there.

Waits for my yes

I read the draft and change it if I want to. He sends the version I approved, and he sends it once.

Keeps my reminders

He brings each reminder to me when it falls due. If I snooze it, he brings it back later.

Reads what I attach

PDFs, Word documents, CSV files and plain text, up to five files with a message.

Remembers what I agree to

If I mention a preference, he asks before he keeps it. I can see everything he remembers and delete any of it.

The rules he works by

02 / RULES
  1. Nothing leaves without my approval.
  2. An approval covers the exact text. If one character changes, he asks me again.
  3. Each approved email goes out once, even if a step has to run again.
  4. Text inside a document, an email or a web page reaches him marked as material to read, and he flags anything in it that reads like an order.
  5. He adds nothing to his memory of me without my agreement.
  6. He keeps a record of everything he does.

How I work with him

03 / IN PRACTICE

Each morning, Ola reads my calendar and my unread mail and sets out the day: the meetings, the messages waiting for a reply, the tasks that fall due, and anything he has prepared for my approval.

Through the day I write to him the way I would write to an assistant. If I ask him to email someone, he drafts the email and waits. If I ask for a meeting, he prepares the invitation and waits. I read the draft, change it if I want to, and approve it. Then it goes out. If the text changes after I approve it, my approval lapses and he asks me again.

He keeps my reminders and brings each one to me once, when it falls due. If I mention a preference, he asks whether to keep it, and keeps it when I say yes.

Ola runs on my own server. The desk where I work with him is private.
